Bug-fix release on top of 0.19.0. It's **not independently reviewed**.

- **Microphone noise works on more phones.** Before, it often failed with an error. Many phones
  return a short silence while the microphone starts, which failed the health test. Some don't
  support the "unprocessed" audio source.
  - The first 300 ms are now dropped.
  - The app falls back to other microphone sources and sample rates.
  - If the microphone only gives silence, the message tells you to check Android's microphone
    access toggle in quick settings.
- **Strict chats with post-quantum keys:** a "share retired" answer signed with ML-DSA is
  about 7 KB, and the app only read the first 4 KB of it. The chat was therefore never removed
  after its share was retired. It failed safe: nothing was wrongly deleted.
- **Smaller attack surface:**
  - The NFC card service is off while NFC pairing is paused.
  - The relay's authority-key answer is size-capped.

## Install

Download `otp-pager-0.19.1.apk` below and install it over 0.19.0 on **both** phones. It
updates in place and keeps chats, pads and pairings.

## Server / Portainer

No relay changes. If you already redeployed for 0.19.0, there's nothing to do.

## What to test

- On the USB pairing screen, tap **Add microphone noise**. It should say "Microphone noise
  added · health tests passed". If it says the microphone only gave silence, check the
  microphone toggle in quick settings and try again.
